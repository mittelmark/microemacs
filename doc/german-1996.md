There isn’t an official “100 most important words” list—the reform changed spelling rules and many thousands of forms. For a learner, these **100 high-value examples** cover the changes you’re most likely to encounter. The **left form is the old spelling; the bold right form is the current standard**. A few compound or phrase spellings were adjusted again in 2006, so I’ve used today’s standard forms. <citation src="1,2,5"></citation>

**1. ß → ss after a short vowel**  
The ß in these older spellings became **ss**; ß remains after long vowels and diphthongs, as in *Straße* and *Fuß*. <citation src="2,5"></citation>

1. daß → **dass** — that  
2. muß → **muss** — must  
3. Fluß → **Fluss** — river  
4. Kuß → **Kuss** — kiss  
5. Schloß → **Schloss** — castle  
6. Paß → **Pass** — pass  
7. Maß → **Maß** — measure (unchanged)  
8. biß → **biss** — bit  
9. riß → **riss** — tore  
10. aß → **aß** — ate (unchanged)  
11. vergaß → **vergaß** — forgot (unchanged)  
12. ließ → **ließ** — let/left (unchanged)  
13. küßte → **küsste** — kissed  
14. haßte → **hasste** — hated  
15. paßte → **passte** — fit  
16. faßte → **fasste** — grasped  
17. wußte → **wusste** — knew  
18. mußte → **musste** — had to  
19. mußten → **mussten** — had to (plural)  
20. läßt → **lässt** — lets  
21. bißchen → **bisschen** — a little  
22. häßlich → **hässlich** — ugly  
23. naß → **nass** — wet  
24. blaß → **blass** — pale  
25. Schluß → **Schluss** — end

31. Ausschluß → **Ausschluss** — exclusion  
32. Ausfluß → **Ausfluss** — discharge  
33. Überfluß → **Überfluss** — abundance  
34. Mißverständnis → **Missverständnis** — misunderstanding  
35. mißachten → **missachten** — disregard
